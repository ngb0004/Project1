# Cornell / Chi Phi case: human-detail and online-framing research

Researched 2026-10-09. Every quote below was copied from the page text as fetched on 2026-10-09 (raw text saved in `scratchpad/pages/<id>.txt`). Quote format: «verbatim text» [source id]. No accused student is named. Where a source names an accused student, only name-free fragments are quoted.

Labels used:
- **HER ACCOUNT**: what Jane Doe said (to police, to investigators, in texts, or in her lawsuit). Not tested in any court.
- **REPORTED**: reported by a news outlet from documents or interviews, but not a court or official finding.
- **ESTABLISHED**: confirmed by an official record, a ruling, or several independent outlets agreeing on a document's contents (for example, that a message exists), not that an allegation is true.

## 1. The night and her account

### 1a. Before the fraternity house
- **HER ACCOUNT (lawsuit)**: She drank vodka at her sorority house, went to two downtown bars, then walked alone to Chi Phi around 11 p.m. to see a friend.
  - «Around 11 PM, Plaintiff decided to leave Lot 10 and stumbled alone to» [complaint]
  - «Plaintiff was visibly intoxicated as she had consumed about 10 standard drinks within the past 3 hours.» [complaint]
- **HER ACCOUNT (2024, per NYT summary of the Title IX file)**: She put her drunkenness at about 5 or 6 out of 10 when the first encounter began, rising to near 10 by the end.
  - «In 2024, she put her initial level of inebriation at about a “5 or 6” out of 10 when that first encounter began. By the end of the night, she said, it was near a 10» [nyt-takeaways]
- **REPORTED (AP, from the Title IX panel's findings)**: She went at the invitation of a fraternity member she had long liked; the panel found she first consented to sex with him and one friend.
  - «The woman had been invited there by a fraternity member for whom she had long held strong romantic feelings and sexual interest, according to the disciplinary panel’s findings.» [ap-bait]
  - «The panel found that she initially consented to sex with that man and one of his friends» [ap-bait]

### 1b. Inside the house
- **HER ACCOUNT (Nov. 2024 sworn statement, quoted by NBC)**: She agreed to a threesome at first, then felt more and more incapacitated, had gaps in memory, and could not reach her phone.
  - «Jane Doe told police in part that she had agreed to an initial sexual encounter with two of the men, described being encouraged to take drugs and said she did not remember some parts of the night.» [nbc-statement]
  - «Adding to my feelings of isolation was the fact that my phone was across the room and I felt like I couldn’t get to it» [nbc-statement]
  - She heard two men «say something about ‘having a turn.’» [nbc-statement]
- **HER ACCOUNT (sworn statement, quoted by ABC)**: When a group of men came in she hid under the covers; she said "no to the foursome"; she said the later encounter "felt like coercion."
  - «I was instantly horrified, and I remember squealing out loud and running across the room and hiding under the covers on another bed» [abc-statement]
  - «To me, that felt like coercion,» [abc-statement]
  - «I would say that it was the most intoxicated I've ever been in my life. I felt like I was unable to make any decision because of how intoxicated I was» [abc-statement]
- **HER ACCOUNT: why she took more ketamine** (this line is in the six-page statement the DA saw):
  - «I had become extremely uncomfortable with how this night was turning out so my thought was, if I do Ketamine again maybe I will pass out. My thought was that if I passed out and these men had sex with me, then they could be charged with Rape» [abc-statement]
- **HER ACCOUNT: men snorting drugs off her body**:
  - «She recalled being so "extremely tired" she simply uttered, "Sure" when a group of men asked to snort ketamine off of her body, according to her statement.» [abc-statement]
- **HER ACCOUNT: being hit**. The lawsuit says one man slapped her repeatedly, leaving bruises. The NYT reports she photographed fading bruises.
  - «she took a picture of fading bruises on her buttocks that she alleged were caused by one of the men hitting her.» [nyt-takeaways]
- **HER ACCOUNT (lawsuit): ketamine.** She had never used it before, says she was pressured into it, and that by the end she was "completely incapacitated."
  - Name-free summary of complaint para. 153: two of the men "pressured" her into snorting the alleged ketamine, which the complaint calls «a drug she had never done before.» [complaint]
  - «By this point, Plaintiff was completely incapacitated.» [complaint]
- **How many men?** The lawsuit names **7** men. Cornell police's first report says she reported **8-10**. Her sworn statement describes rumors of an "eightsome" and of ten people.
  - «A female undergraduate student reported that she was recently raped by 8-10 men at the Chi Phi fraternity house.» [abc-sexdoll] (ESTABLISHED that police wrote this down; ABC quotes the police record.)
  - «The lawsuit seeks to hold all seven men liable for rape.» [ap-bait]

  - «claiming there was “free pussy” upstairs, referring to Plaintiff thereat.» [complaint]

  - The chat had «nearly 60 members» [abc-damage]. (ABC's Oct. 2 piece says "more than 50".)

### 1e. Evidence she gathered, then the police report (Nov. 2024)
- **REPORTED (NYT, from the Title IX file)**: She told a campus clinic she got an infection "in a traumatic way," kept her dress unwashed, photographed a hickey and bruises, and had a pelvic exam at an ER the day before going to police.
  - «She didn’t wash the dress she had been wearing that night, to save potential evidence.» [nyt-takeaways]

- **The police interview (Nov. 14, 2024)**: about 120 pages of transcript, condensed by the police investigator into a six-page statement that she signed. The DA based his decision on the six pages.
  - «The transcript of their conversation stretches about 120 pages. It was condensed by the police investigator into a six-page official statement that the woman signed.» [nyt-takeaways]
  - «“I can say with 100% confidence I was raped,” she told the police investigator about what happened by the end of that night, in a statement that did not make it into the six-page summary report.» [nyt-takeaways]
  - In the same interview, about the start of the night, she called it «“pretty OK”» [nyt-takeaways]
  - She also said: «I was a sex doll for all intents and purposes, which is sad.» [nyt-takeaways]
